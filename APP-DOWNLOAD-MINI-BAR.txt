OptiTrade App Download Mini-Bar Patch

Adds a small dismissible Android app download bar to:
- homepage
- dashboard
- markets/trading/portfolio/investment pages
- deposit/withdraw/transfer/transaction pages
- profile/security/support/integrations/KYC pages

Behavior:
- fixed at the bottom, compact and responsive
- Download App points to /downloads/OptiTrade.apk
- if APK is missing or 0 bytes, sends user to /download-app.html instead of a broken download
- X closes the banner
- dismissal is remembered for 7 days in that browser
- automatically hidden inside the installed OptiTrade Android app

Files added:
public/css/app-download-banner.css
public/js/app-download-banner.js

Files updated:
the listed public HTML pages only.

No .env, database, API keys, signing keys, node_modules, or private credentials are included.
