OPTITRADE 2.11.1 — CUSTOM CONFIRMATION DIALOGS

All browser-native confirmation/prompt/alert boxes used by the current runtime UI have been replaced with styled OptiTrade dialogs.

Covered actions include:
- Deposit sent confirmation
- Deposit Accept & Credit / decline / historical correction
- Move Trade Reserve to Available Balance
- Cancel pending investment
- Investment settlement / early settlement
- Withdrawal approval
- Admin balance reversal and high-value adjustments
- Receiving-wallet deletion
- Connected-wallet removal
- Banner deletion
- Welcome / Quick Help reset
- Telegram removal
- Kraken Close & Reconcile
- Kraken CANCEL ALL emergency confirmation
- Other informational alerts previously shown by the browser

The dialogs are responsive on desktop/mobile and support:
- Confirm / Cancel
- Destructive-action styling
- Required input/reason fields
- X / outside-click / Escape dismissal

No database reset required.
