OPTITRADE 2.9.1 — TRADE WITH OPTITRADE REVIEW SUMMARY

Before a customer can submit Trade with OptiTrade, the app now shows a Review Summary.

The summary includes:
- Amount
- Current available USD wallet
- Wallet balance after reservation
- Market preference
- Preferred pair
- Customer note
- Pending Admin Review status

The customer must tick:
  "I have reviewed the amount, market preference and reservation details."

Only then is Confirm & Submit enabled.

Nothing is reserved or submitted while the review modal is open.

When the customer confirms, the existing server-side Trade with OptiTrade flow runs:
- funds move from Wallet USD to Trading reserve
- request becomes Pending
- assigned admin / Super Admin Attention Center receives the urgent request
- Telegram urgent alert is sent when configured

No database reset required.
