OPTITRADE 2.10.0 — PERSISTENT TRADE RESERVE

Trade with OptiTrade now has a persistent Trade Reserve.

CUSTOMER VIEW
The Trade with OptiTrade section shows:
- Total Trade Reserve
- Committed to open trades
- Free to Re-trade / Move
- Realized P/L
- Available USD wallet
- Active / pending managed request counts

NEW TRADE FUNDING
When a customer submits a new Trade with OptiTrade request:
1. Free Trade Reserve is used first.
2. Available USD wallet supplies only the difference.
3. The review summary shows the exact source breakdown before submission.

SETTLEMENT
When an administrator settles Trade with OptiTrade:
- principal does NOT automatically return to Available Balance;
- profit/loss is applied inside the Trade Reserve;
- the resulting value remains in Trade Reserve;
- the customer can immediately reuse free reserve for another Trade with OptiTrade request.

This also applies to a successfully closed/reconciled Kraken-routed Trade with OptiTrade position.

DECLINED MANAGED REQUEST
A declined pending Trade with OptiTrade request releases its principal back to FREE Trade Reserve rather than automatically sending it to Available Balance.

MOVE TO AVAILABLE BALANCE
Customer can click:
  Move to Available Balance

The customer chooses an amount and enters the 4-digit transaction PIN.

Only FREE Trade Reserve can move.
Funds committed to pending/active trades cannot move.

This is an internal balance move, not an external withdrawal.
Total account value does not change.

SELF-DIRECTED TRADING
Self-Directed Trading keeps its existing settlement behavior.
This persistent reserve applies to Trade with OptiTrade.

REALIZED P/L
The reserve section shows cumulative realized P/L from settled Trade with OptiTrade requests.
Active trades do not show realized P/L until settlement.

NO DATABASE RESET REQUIRED.

New managed trade columns are added non-destructively:
- wallet_funded_amount
- reserve_funded_amount

Keep:
  .env
  database/
  node_modules/

Then run:
  npm run audit
  npm run smoke-test
  npm start
