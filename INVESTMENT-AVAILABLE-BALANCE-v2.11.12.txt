OptiTrade v2.11.12 — Investment Plans Use Available Balance

Investment funding flow:
- Investment plans now use Available Balance (USD) directly.
- Submitting a plan immediately moves the requested amount from Wallet/Available Balance to Investment Reserve.
- The reduced Available Balance is returned by the API immediately.
- The Investment Plans page now shows:
  • Available to invest
  • Reserved in investments
- Cancelling or rejecting a pending request releases the reserved amount back to Available Balance.
- USD investment settlement returns principal + result directly to Available Balance without requiring an external crypto price.

Admin notification:
- Assigned admin is notified when a customer submits an investment plan.
- If no Sub-Admin owns the customer, the Super Admin is used automatically.
- The missing money() formatter crash is fixed and the admin-notification call is protected.

Also preserved:
- Deposit response-first/no-hang server behavior from v2.11.9.
- Invisible dashboard banner slider/dot control while auto-slide remains active.

- Does not include database/
